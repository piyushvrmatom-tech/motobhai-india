Frequently Asked Questions

How do I plan a trip?
Open the app, tap "New Trip", enter your start and end points, and Moto Bhai will generate a route, fuel estimate, and shareable Google Maps link.

Is the app free?
Yes, core trip planning features are free to use.

How do I share my itinerary?
After generating a trip, tap the share icon to send your Google Maps link or PDF itinerary.

How do I delete my account or data?
Email us at support@motobhai.app with the subject "Delete Account" from your registered email address, and we'll process your request within 7 days.
